# 🏋️‍♂️ Friday: Chest Push & Hamstring Chain Balance

*   **Total Time:** 15 Minutes
*   **Structure:** Superset format. Complete **3 rounds** total. Rest 45 seconds at the end of each full round.
*   **Equipment:** Yoga mat, Heavy Dumbbells.

---

### 🤝 Pairing 1: Core-Engaged Push & Hip-Hinge Pull (6 Minutes)

#### 1. Standard Floor Push-Ups
*   **Reps/Time:** 10 to 12 reps.
*   **Directions:** Assume a high plank position on your mat, hands slightly wider than shoulders. Lower your chest down while keeping your elbows tucked at a 45-degree angle. Push back up securely.
*   **Why:** Pairs chest, front shoulder, and tricep power with full front-body stability.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/standard_floor_push-ups.jpg)

#### 2. Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs)
*   **Reps/Time:** 12 slow reps (3 seconds down, 1 second up). Use heavy dumbbells.
*   **Directions:** Stand tall with weights in front of your thighs. Keep a soft bend in your knees. Push your hips straight backward, sliding the weights down your shins until you feel a deep hamstring stretch. Stand.
*   **Why:** Targets the upper hamstrings and glutes without overloading the knees.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/dumbbell_romanian_deadlifts.jpg)

---

### 🛏️ Pairing 2: Isolated Press & Eccentric Control (6 Minutes)

#### 3. Floor Dumbbell Chest Press
*   **Reps/Time:** 12 repetitions. Use heavy dumbbells.
*   **Directions:** Lie flat on your back on your mat with knees bent and feet flat. Hold your weights straight above your chest. Lower down smoothly until your upper arms rest on the floor, then press back up.
*   **Why:** Builds pressing power through a shoulder-safe range of motion limited by the floor.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/floor_dumbbell_chest_press.jpg)

#### 4. Yoga Mat Hamstring Walkouts
*   **Reps/Time:** 6 full walkouts (Out and back equals 1 repetition).
*   **Directions:** Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat. Lift your hips into a bridge. Keep your hips up and take small, alternating heel-steps forward until legs are straight, then walk them back in.
*   **Why:** Develops eccentric hamstring capacity, preventing hamstring strains and supporting the back of the knee.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/yoga_mat_hamstring_walkouts.jpg)

---

### 🔥 Finisher: Isometric Burnout (3 Minutes)

#### 5. Plank-to-Push-Up Hold
*   **Reps/Time:** 10-15 second low hover, immediately followed by a 30-second high plank hold.
*   **Directions:** Drop to the bottom of a push-up position (hovering 2 inches off the mat) and hold it. When your chest tires, push up immediately into a locked high plank and hold until time runs out.
*   **Why:** Maximizes muscular endurance and completely exhausts shoulder and chest stabilizers.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/plank-to-push-up_hold.jpg)
