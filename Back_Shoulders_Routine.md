# 🏋️‍♂️ Thursday: Upper Back & Shoulder Posture

*   **Total Time:** 15 Minutes
*   **Structure:** Complete the circuit **3 times** total. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.
*   **Equipment:** Elastic band, Light Dumbbells, Casual Barbell, Heavy Dumbbells.

---

### 👐 Phase 1: Scapular & Rear Shoulder Stability (5 Minutes)

#### 1. Band Pull-Aparts
*   **Reps/Time:** 20 slow repetitions.
*   **Directions:** Stand holding an elastic band out at shoulder height with straight arms. Pull your hands wide apart, stretching the band until it touches your chest. Intentionally squeeze your shoulder blades.
*   **Why:** Targets the rhomboids and rear delts to re-align shoulders and correct posture.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/band_pull-aparts.jpg)

#### 2. Prone Dumbbell Y Raises
*   **Reps/Time:** 12 controlled repetitions (Use LIGHT weights).
*   **Directions:** Lie face down on your mat holding your light dumbbells. Extend your arms out at a 45-degree angle (forming a "Y"). Keep thumbs pointed up. Raise your arms straight up using your upper back.
*   **Why:** Isolates the lower trapezius muscle, which locks down the shoulder blades during overhead movements.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/prone_dumbbell_y_raises.jpg)

---

### 🪵 Phase 2: Heavy Back Pulling & Balance (5 Minutes)

#### 3. Barbell Bent-Over Rows
*   **Reps/Time:** 12 slow repetitions (Use your empty casual barbell).
*   **Directions:** Hinge forward at your hips until your torso is nearly parallel to the floor. Hold the barbell with an overhand grip. Pull the bar smoothly up to your lower ribs, keeping your elbows tucked tight.
*   **Why:** Develops total back thickness and forces static spinal bracing.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/barbell_bent-over_rows.jpg)

#### 4. Heavy Dumbbell Farmer Carries
*   **Reps/Time:** Walk continuously for 45 seconds.
*   **Directions:** Grab your heavy dumbbells. Stand up perfectly straight with shoulders packed down and back. Walk slowly and smoothly in a straight line, ensuring the weights do not sway or wobble.
*   **Why:** Builds grip strength and intense, full-body anti-rotational stability.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/heavy_dumbbell_farmer_carries.jpg)

---

### 🚀 Phase 3: Overhead Shoulder Track (5 Minutes)

#### 5. Barbell Overhead Strict Press
*   **Reps/Time:** 10 strict repetitions.
*   **Directions:** Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Clean the barbell to your collarbones. Brace your core tightly and press the bar straight overhead until your elbows lock out completely. Lower slowly.
*   **Why:** Coordinates the core and shoulders to safely execute overhead extensions.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/barbell_overhead_strict_press.jpg)

#### 6. Banded Scapular Wall Slides
*   **Reps/Time:** 10 slow slides up and down.
*   **Directions:** Put your elastic band around your wrists. Place your forearms flat against a wall, keeping your wrists wide to hold band tension. Slide your arms up the wall into a "V" shape and return.
*   **Why:** Dynamically teaches the shoulder blades to stabilize safely against friction.
*   ![Visual Hint](images/banded_scapular_wall_slides.jpg)
